# PawChorale source and release terms

PawChorale is a compilation of rendered choral audio, MIDI labels, MusicXML,
and metadata derived from the exact source editions listed in
`source-rights-manifest.csv`. There is no blanket research-only license for the
entire compilation. The edition-level terms recorded in that manifest remain
controlling for material derived from each source.

## Edition-level terms in version 1.0.0

- 172 works are marked `CPDL`. Preserve the source attribution and copyright
  notices, the right of recipients to redistribute, perform, record, and modify
  covered editions, and the other conditions of the CPDL License:
  https://www.cpdl.org/wiki/index.php/CPDL_license
- IDs 5, 140, and 145 are marked `Creative Commons Attribution
  Non-Commercial`. Preserve attribution and the non-commercial restriction
  recorded for those exact editions. Consult their source URLs in
  `source-rights-manifest.csv` before reuse.
- 25 works are marked `Public Domain` by their source editions. The manifest
  preserves provenance; users remain responsible for jurisdiction-specific
  public-domain determinations.

The archived audio, MIDI, and MusicXML are transformations prepared for the
PawChorale release. Source titles, editors, CPDL numbers, edition markers, and
URLs are provided in `source-rights-manifest.csv`. Do not remove those notices
or impose downstream terms that conflict with an applicable source license.
